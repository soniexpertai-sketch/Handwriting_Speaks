## 7. Tone of voice

- **Warm, empowering, and reassuring** — speaks to hopes and worries (esp. parents).
- **Direct address** in second person; the capitalised **"YOU"** is a signature device.
- **Aphoristic & quotable** — short declarative truths ("believe the handwriting").
- **Lightly scientific / credible** — "graphologist", "scientific approach",
  "detailed report" — authority without heavy jargon.
- **Curiosity-driven hooks** — questions ("Is your child facing any of these
  problems?", "What can we do for you?") and reveals ("KNOW NOW").
- **Action-oriented** — "PLEASE DM", "Book your Customised Consultation", "CALL FOR DETAILS".
- **Indian-English register**, inclusive audience lists (students → home-makers → business owners).
- **Do:** be encouraging, plain-spoken, specific about outcomes.
- **Don't:** sound clinical, fatalistic, or make absolute personality verdicts; avoid
  fortune-telling / superstition framing — keep it "insight + guided correction".
